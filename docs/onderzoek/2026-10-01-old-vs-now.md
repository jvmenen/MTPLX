# Old versus now: Bink's local model setup, end to end (1 October 2026)

Question: on identical workloads, how does the local model setup Bink ran last week compare with the setup in production since the night of 30 September? One interleaved series, fresh server per run, cool machine at the start of every run.

## Summary

- **Faster on every speed metric, no regressions found.** Agent conversation over `/v1/messages` -68% (16.8 to 5.4 s, retries 4 to 0), decode +34 to +61% depending on text type (largest on Dutch with thinking off: 55 to 89 tok/s), cold prefill of 8K and 22K tokens -31% and -21%, scoring route p50 -38%, the real agent task (lus2) -38% median duration, 8 of 8 green.
- **Memory is the same.** Weights +1.0 GiB (BF16 MTP head), but footprint at the end of the series -0.7 GiB and footprint peak -1.3 GiB (median), MLX peak -0.5 GiB. Run-to-run spread is about as large as these differences: no meaningful change.
- **New capability:** first-token logprobs for the classifier (p50 303 ms, accuracy 0.733); the old server refuses that route (HTTP 400).
- **Thermal:** every run started at level 0. Run 1 of OLD stayed at level 0 throughout; the other three reached level 2 in the second half (long-prompt and/or lus2 phase). The decode-heavy phases (meet.py, prose) were at level 0 for 90% or more of the time in all four runs. Prefill was not affected by level 2 (OLD 22K cold: 12.87 s at level 0, 12.88 s at level 2). NOW's lus2 runs were at level 2 more often than OLD's (3 of 4 against 2 of 4), so the lus2 gain is, if anything, understated.

## 1. Setup

- **Hardware:** M5 Pro, 64 GB, 100 W charger. Swap 6.9 GB at the start, no growth during the series (abort threshold +2 GB never reached). One unrelated small MLX helper (FluidVoice) was resident, as during earlier measurements.
- **OLD (last week):** MTPLX 2.11.3 from the Homebrew venv `venv-2.11.3`, started as `python -P -m mtplx.server.openai` with exactly the production arguments of last week (`mtplx-originele-args.txt`: depth 2, turbo, `capture_commit`, `linear-gdn-from-conv-tape`, 4-bit draft LM head, sampling 0.6 / 0.95 / 20, `local_qwen36` template, SSD session cache on), no extra env. Model `Youssofal--Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-MTPLX-Optimized-Balance` (int4 MTP head). This is the "old" arm of final test 3.
- **NOW (production since the night of 30 September):** the launch command from the Bink platform config, verbatim: `mtplx serve --model <pack> --port 8000 --no-auth --mtp --yes` with `PYTHONPATH` on the `perf/definitief` worktree (a40b636d, clean) and the production env: `MTPLX_BATCH_INVARIANT_PREFILL=1`, `MTPLX_MTP_HISTORY_CACHE_ONLY=1`, `MTPLX_PREFILL_CHUNK_SIZE_DENSE=4096`, `MTPLX_PREFILL_CHUNK_SIZE_REPAGE=4096`, `MTPLX_A3B_MOE_PREFILL_COMBINE=1`, `MTPLX_SESSION_HEAD_ANCHOR=1`, `MTPLX_SSD_INCREMENTAL_ENCODE=1`, `MTPLX_POSTCOMMIT_AFTER_RESPONSE=1`, `MTPLX_PERSIST_QUEUE_MAX_GB=4`, `MTPLX_THINKING_BUDGET=6144`, FR-Spec (`MTPLX_FRSPEC_DRAFT=1`, `bink-64k.npy`, `MTPLX_FRSPEC_LEGACY=1`) and `MTPLX_VERIFY_ASYNC_CHUNK_LAYERS=8`. Model `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-MTPLX-Balance-bf16mtp-yb` (same body, BF16 MTP head). `mtplx serve` launched the server with the same flags as OLD's argument file (checked in the process argv); the only difference is `--ssd-session-cache-max-size auto` instead of `100GB`.
- **Only addition to both:** `--ssd-session-cache-dir <fresh empty directory per run>`. The workloads send the same conversations every run, so without isolation run 2 would restore from run 1's SSD cache (and NOW would share production's cache).
- **Order and protocol:** OLD, NOW, OLD, NOW, each on a fresh server on port 8000, under the GPU slot for the whole series (one model loaded at a time, server process gone before the next start). Before each run: 60 s pause, then wait for thermal pressure level 0 (`notifyutil -g com.apple.system.thermalpressurelevel`; it was 0 every time). Thermal level and swap logged every 2 s. Series from 05:00 to 05:26, about 7 minutes per run.
- **Clean runs:** after every workload `requests_completed` in `/health` matched the requests sent (plus OLD's 4 internal retries in meet.py, minus its one refused first-token request), and the pid on the port never changed. No foreign traffic. Server logs: no tracebacks during the workloads; OLD's two logs end with one traceback each from the graceful-shutdown timeout at stop.

### Workloads (same scripts, same order, for both)

1. **meet.py** (`~/Dev/laya-nl/modeltest`): warm-up; 12 greedy chat prompts of 128 tokens; agent conversation of 6 turns via `/v1/messages` with tools, streaming, temperature 0 (3 long tool results of ~2K tokens new text, 3 short); classifier: 60 of the 240 scoring prompts via the scoring route (`echo`, `max_tokens` 0, top-20 logprobs) and via first-token logprobs (`max_tokens` 1); decode 256 tokens at temperature 0, twice; memory after each step.
2. **prose.py** (copy of `frspec36/bench36.py`): reasoning on, server sampling, 1,200 tokens: 3 code, 3 English, 3 Dutch prompts (2 with a Dutch knowledge-base document as source); plus 2 Dutch prompts with thinking off (800 tokens; zero reasoning characters produced on both servers). Decode tok/s from the server's own decode time (`/v1/mtplx/snapshot`), checked against a client-side rate (same within 1%).
3. **lang.py:** 2 rounds of: cold prompt of 8,000 tokens of real knowledge-base markdown (8,050 prompt tokens with the template) behind a unique session header, then 2 s later a follow-up turn on the same prefix (first turn, fixed assistant answer, new question); the same at 22,000 tokens (22,050). Reasoning on, `max_tokens` 8. TTFT = first reasoning or content delta at the client. Both servers restored 8,045 / 22,045 tokens from cache on the warm turn.
4. **lus2.py** (`thinking-proef`), twice per run: a real coding-agent task with Claude Code's tool schemas executed for real in a throwaway project (find why the tests fail, fix the source, run the tests), `/compact` after 6 requests, server sampling.

Notes on the table:

- The model weights differ by 1.0 GiB (27.61 against 28.64 GiB, the BF16 MTP head); that accounts for half of the higher footprint after load. Once the session bank fills, the totals converge (bank 13.4 to 14.0 GiB on OLD, 12.6 to 12.8 GiB on NOW at the end of the run).
- The greedy and decode rows in meet.py are temperature 0 and short; the prose rows (server sampling, reasoning on, 1,200 tokens) are closer to real Bink traffic.
- The decode gain combines the BF16 MTP head (higher acceptance), FR-Spec with the Bink vocabulary (cheaper draft step: draft share of decode time 15.4% to 12.6%) and the async verify chunk. This series does not separate them; see [frspec-qwen36](2026-09-30-frspec-qwen36.md) and [host-overhead-qwen36](2026-09-30-host-overhead-qwen36.md) for the individual effects.
- The lus2 task is sampled, so the number of requests varies per attempt (OLD 9 to 15, NOW 12 to 13). Seconds per request and TTFT per request are less sensitive to that than the total duration.

## 3. Thermal pressure

| Run | Level 2 reached | Share of samples at level 2 | Phases at level 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| OLD 1 | no | 0% | none |
| NOW 1 | yes, after 228 s | 15% | lus2 (both attempts) |
| OLD 2 | yes, after 246 s | 32% | long prompts, lus2 (both), last 2% of prose |
| NOW 2 | yes, after 177 s | 37% | long prompts, lus2 (both), last 10% of prose |

The `meet.py` workload ran at level 0 in all four runs; prose at level 0 for at least 90% of its time. The long-prompt results show no thermal effect on prefill (OLD 12.87 s at level 0 against 12.88 s at level 2; NOW 10.19 s at level 0 against 10.06 s at level 2). lus2 ran at level 2 in three of NOW's four attempts and in two of OLD's four.
